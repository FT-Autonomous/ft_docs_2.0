# Hall of Fame

Celebrating the people who built Formula Trinity Autonomous, and kept it going from one team to the next.

## Autonomous Leadership

Years below are **team seasons**, not calendar years: 2022/23 means the team that worked towards the 2023 competition. Elections often happened before the previous team's competition, so an election date is not necessarily the start of someone's term.

| Season | Captain / Team Lead | CTO / Technical Leadership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2018/19 and 2019/20 | Early appointments still to be confirmed | Early appointments still to be confirmed |
| 2020/21 | Founding group listed below | Individual Captain / CTO appointments still to be confirmed |
| 2021/22 | Katherine Hardgrave, then titled Head of Engineering | Daniel Flood, CTO |
| 2022/23 | Emran Yasser Moustafa | Harvey Brezina Conniffe; Chinaza Uzoukwu from November 2022 |
| 2023/24 | Advik Bahadur | Chinaza Uzoukwu |
| 2024/25 | Cleavon Castelino | Chinaza Uzoukwu |
| 2025/26 | Jake Casserly | Dhruv Ranajit Choudhury |
| 2026/27 | Joel Jojan, AI Captain; Adam Walsh, Systems (ADS) Captain | Conor Moran, CTO |

The job titles have changed along the way. In 2021/22, **Head of Engineering was the role later called Captain**, with CTO a separate technical role. More recent teams also use Head of Engineering for CTO. The captions keep the titles used for each season.[^titles]

## 2018/19 and 2019/20: The Early Project

The team's entry in the official 2022 Formula Student programme dates the initial Autonomous project to **late 2018**, before its restart in 2020. The names and exact roles of the early leaders still need confirming; they have not been filled in from the IC committee or assumed from later roles.[^early]

## 2020/21: Founding Group

**Andrew Dai, Jakub Pyszka and Senan Stanley** restarted the project in 2020. Andrew also credits **Katherine Hardgrave** as a co-founder of the Autonomous racing group. They belong here together, rather than being fitted into Captain and CTO titles that the available records do not establish.[^founders]

## Keeping This Page Up to Date

Add a new **season**, with the person's name, role and a confirmed headshot in `docs/media/hof/`. If someone leaves partway through a season, keep both names and record the handover rather than replacing the outgoing member.

Use the original headshot without another crop. The cards fit the whole image inside the thumbnail, and an image can be opened at full size. A `{Headshot: Name}` box means a confirmed photo is still needed, not that the person's role is uncertain. Early leadership appointments are marked separately in the overview.

Keep the supporting appointment or handover reference, even when the photo comes from a newer season. Photo origins and remaining checks are recorded in `research/hall-of-fame.md` in the repository. If someone would prefer not to have their photo or entry here, remove it.
