# Archive

Completed projects and former departments live here so their work remains useful without being confused with the current setup.

## Hardware, 2025/26

[Hardware](../../departments/hardware/overview.md) covered the competition sensor mount and the mechanical/electronics work on Rigby. The department finished in August 2026. Its mount design, original wiring notes, print references and sketches are retained together.

The electrical pages are the original project references, not a current Rigby installation guide. Board choices, firmware and control routes have since changed. Their placement here preserves that history without rewriting another team's technical notes.

**Rigby is still active** and now has its own [section under Software](../../departments/rigby/overview.md), including current mechanical work, the FT-Rigby repository and testing status.

Hardware's existing page URLs are retained so older links still work. The navigation, rather than the old URL spelling, identifies it as archived.
