# Autonomous Hall of Fame Research

Checked: 29 September 2026. Branch: `conor/halloffame`.

This is the maintenance record for `docs/hall_of_fame.md`, not a complete export of the team's communications. Do not turn an election nomination, an IC role, a current profile title or a photo filename into a historical appointment without supporting evidence.

## Coverage

- Searched the former Autonomous channel, now `a_autonomous_software` (`C018H460279`), and `1_general` (`CMT82PSG3`) with season-specific date ranges and Captain, CTO, Head of Engineering, committee, election and handover terms.
- Read the public archived `d_autonomous_committee_organisation` history (`C024HS5ENPP`), relevant announcement threads and the June 2021 Autonomous presentation (`F0256L9551A`).
- Followed relevant records in `a_business`, `a_path-planning` and `b_website`, and the headshot folders announced in general.
- Cross-checked the official Formula Student programme, current Formula Trinity member page and named members' own public accounts where needed.
- The old software channel was created in August 2020; general's accessible history starts in September 2019. A separate Autonomous Slack workspace was used before members moved into this workspace in 2021. That separate workspace's history was not inspected.
- Searches and retained announcements do not establish every appointment from 2018 onwards. The page explicitly leaves early appointments unconfirmed instead of claiming historical exhaustion.
